🌐 Why do people share things online?

People create websites, videos, pictures, and messages for different reasons.

🛍️

Advertising

To make us interested in buying something.

BUY OR TRY
💬

Opinion

To share what someone thinks or likes.

I THINK…
📚

Information

To help people learn something.

A FACT

🔑 Five Important Keywords

Learn these words to become a great detective.

🌐

Online Content

Things we see or read on the internet.

Example: videos and pictures
📢

Advertisement

A message that tries to sell something.

“Buy our new toy!”
💬

Opinion

What someone thinks or feels.

“Cats are the best pets!”
📚

Information

Facts or details that help us learn.

“Cats have whiskers.”
🎯

Purpose

The reason something was made.

To sell, share ideas, or teach

🛡️ Be a Smart Online Detective!

Use these simple reminders when you see something online.

🔎
Ask yourself: Why was this made?
🛍️
An advertisement tries to make you want to buy something.
💭
An opinion is not the same as a fact.
🧑‍🏫
If something seems strange or confusing, ask a trusted adult.
💡 Detective Tip: One website or video can have more than one type of message. Look carefully!
